The three most **load-bearing and least evidenced** bets — not the three most uncertain.

| # | assumption | extracted? |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Anyone outside this project wants the thing | no |
| 2 | The example generalises | no |
| 3 | The cost estimate holds at real volume | half |

routinely reads far healthier than it.
